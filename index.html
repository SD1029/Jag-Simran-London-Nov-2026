The concept

Two hotels.
Two Londons.

The hotel split is intentional: start in Marylebone for the classic first-timer London experience, then move east for Shoreditch's food, markets and nightlife. You only pack up once.

01

Classic London

Royal London, Westminster, Buckingham Palace, Trafalgar Square, Covent Garden and the West End — all from a polished central home base.

02

Neighbourhood London

Shoreditch, Brick Lane, Spitalfields, St Paul's, Borough Market and Tower Bridge — a completely different rhythm.

♥

Romantic London

Slow breakfasts, long walks, double-decker rides upstairs, dressed-up dinners and enough empty space for spontaneous discoveries.

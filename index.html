Churchill + Aethos gives you the greatest contrast. Churchill is your polished, classic West London chapter; Aethos is your energetic East London chapter. Andaz Liverpool Street is a terrific hotel, but it overlaps with Aethos's geography and would reduce the payoff of moving hotels.

Final recommendation

Book the contrast.

Nov 19–21 · Hyatt Regency London – The Churchill. Start the trip in polished Marylebone, close to the West End, Hyde Park, Oxford Street and Bond Street. It gives the opening two nights the classic, refined London feeling you want. citeturn0search6turn0search7

Nov 21–23 · Aethos London Shoreditch. Then move east and let the second half feel completely different: Brick Lane, Spitalfields, Shoreditch, the City and Tower Bridge.

Andaz Liverpool Street is Plan B. It is arguably the more practical hotel because Liverpool Street Station is directly beside it and the property provides easy access to East London and airport rail connections. citeturn0search0turn0search2 But with Aethos already booked for nights 3–4, Churchill creates the better two-neighbourhood story.

The overall philosophy: classic London first, neighbourhood London second; spend on two spectacular dinners and one West End night; use walking and regular buses as part of the sightseeing; keep enough empty space to discover something neither of us planned.

This should feel like a romantic London movie — not a checklist. The red bus, the phone booth, the palace, the Thames, the theatre, the rainy walk, the little café you weren't planning to visit. Those are the moments the itinerary is designed to protect.
